All of these projects are from the UBC Apsc 100/101 courses taken by all first year engineering students at UBC. In this course, we work as teams to develop projects. That said, I modelled all projects presented on this page.


## Apsc 101 Claw Project

We had to design and fabricate a claw that could pick up a variety of objects. I modelled a recreation of our claw, with used parameters to help finalise the dimensions of our project.

![CAD model of Claw]({{ '/assets/images/projects/APSC-Claw.png' }})
*CAD model of Claw*

And used this model to create a technical drawing which was part of the project deliverables.

![Claw technical drawing]({{ '/assets/images/projects/CAD-Claw-Drawing.png' }})
*Claw technical drawing*

## Apsc 100 Chess Keychain

We were instructed to design a keychain in CAD (within certain parameters), which would then be 3d printed. I decided to make a half-king half-queen chess piece.

![Keychain CAD]({{ '/assets/images/projects/Chess-Keychain.png' }})

This model may look a bit squished, and that's because the maximum dimensions where a rectangular prism, so I scaled it non-uniformly to fit better in the constraints.

![3d printed keychain]({{ '/assets/images/projects/Real-Keychain.jpg' }})
*3d printed keychain*

## Apsc 100 Cardboard chair

Our team had to design and constructed a cardboard chair that would fit and hold a kid. I took it upon myself to CAD the chair using Fusion360's sheet-metal tools.

![CAD model of cardboard chair]({{ '/assets/images/projects/Cardboard-Chair.png' }})
*CAD model of cardboard chair*

It was made of a single piece of cardboard and was later modified to resemble a thrown.
